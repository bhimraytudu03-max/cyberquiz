🎓 Workshop Lesson: Never Click Unknown Links!

Don't worry — your device is completely safe! This was a simulated exercise for your cybersecurity workshop.

Just by clicking a single link or button, an attacker could silently execute malicious code, steal cookies, or log your personal details.

Rules for Link Safety:
  • Inspect the URL: Check for typos in domain names (e.g., micros0ft.com).
  • Hover Before Clicking: On desktop, hover to see the real destination link.
  • Verify the Source: Never open links from unexpected emails, SMS, or QR codes.
